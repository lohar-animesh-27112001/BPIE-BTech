About the department

The curriculum will cover programming, data structures, software development, artificial intelligence and data science, with equal weight on theoretical concepts and practical work.

Computer systems and classrooms for foundational learning are already in place. Details of faculty, laboratories and the full curriculum will be added after approval.

How the department teaches

Strong technical fundamentals come first, taught through a balance of theory and hands-on work in the computing labs. The aim is a student-friendly environment that supports continuous learning and skill development, in line with academic requirements and regulatory norms.

Areas of focus

Five subject areas run through the program, from first-year foundations to final-year projects.

Programming and software development
Data structures and algorithms
Artificial intelligence and machine learning
Data science and analytics
Computer networks and systems
