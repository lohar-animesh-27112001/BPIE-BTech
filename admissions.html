Admission questions

For anything else, write to bpieadmission@gmail.com.

Is the institute approved by AICTE?

Yes. AICTE, New Delhi has approved the institute’s B.Tech programs for the 2026–27 session. University affiliation (MAKAUT) and the state government NOC are in process.

When do admissions start?

Admissions begin once the state authorities give final clearance. AICTE approval is already in place. Leave your number or email above and we’ll tell you when applications open.

How are students selected?

Through a transparent, merit-based process, as per AICTE and university guidelines. Eligibility, entrance requirements and counselling details will be published here after approval.

How many seats does each program have?

The proposed intake is 60 seats a year in each of the four programs. Final numbers follow university affiliation.

Is hostel accommodation available?

Hostel details have not been announced yet. If a hostel is offered, its fee will be notified along with the fee structure.
