Questions from students and parents

Can’t find your answer? Call the admission desk on +91 94341 01473.

Is the institute approved by AICTE?

Yes. AICTE, New Delhi has approved the institute’s B.Tech programs for the 2026–27 session. University affiliation (MAKAUT) and the state government NOC are in process.

Which programs are offered?

Computer Science & Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Artificial Intelligence, each with a proposed intake of 60 seats a year.

When do admissions start?

Admissions begin once the state authorities give final clearance. Leave your mobile number or email on the admissions page and we’ll let you know when applications open.

What are the fees?

Fees are set as per regulatory norms. The final fee structure will be published after approval from AICTE and the other authorities.

Where is the campus?

At Siromonipur, Bishnupur, in Bankura district, West Bengal.
