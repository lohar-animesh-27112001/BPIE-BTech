Who we are

Bishnupur Public Institute of Engineering – B.Tech is a new engineering institute in Bankura district, set up under Bishnupur Public Education Institute (BPEI). It exists to offer quality technical education, backed by the infrastructure, academic facilities and practical learning that engineering needs.

Classrooms, laboratories, workshops and the library are in place. Development continues in line with AICTE norms, with the focus on strong technical knowledge, practical skills and industry-oriented learning.

Vision and mission

The principles that shape how the institute teaches, and what kind of engineers it aims to develop.

Vision

To become a centre of excellence in engineering education: imparting quality technical knowledge, encouraging innovation, research and creativity, and developing competent, ethical professionals who keep learning as industry and society change.

Mission

  • Provide quality technical education through a structured framework that balances theory with practical application.
  • Develop skilled, competent engineers who can meet the needs of industry and contribute to society.
  • Encourage innovation and creativity through hands-on training, problem-solving and project work.
  • Keep a disciplined, ethical and professional environment that builds responsibility, teamwork and leadership.
  • Keep improving academic and infrastructure facilities in line with modern standards and regulatory guidelines.

Management

Bishnupur Public Education Institute (BPEI) manages the institute. Its management committee sets policy, plans the institute’s development and makes sure it runs in line with regulatory guidelines and educational standards.
