Anti-ragging

The institute has a zero-tolerance policy on ragging, on campus and in the surrounding areas. Campus activity is monitored in line with AICTE and UGC regulations, and students are asked to report any incident to the authorities.

Online undertaking required. Every student and parent must submit an anti-ragging affidavit at www.antiragging.in at the start of each academic session.

Rules and regulations

All registered students follow the academic standards and regulations of AICTE and the affiliating university.

Code of conduct

  • Identity card: carry and display it at all times on campus, in labs, the library and college transport.
  • Dress: formal and neat; lab coats and safety boots are required in workshops.
  • Property: damaging walls, furniture or lab equipment leads to penalties and disciplinary action.
  • Substances: alcohol, tobacco and illegal substances are prohibited and are grounds for expulsion.

Attendance

  • 75% minimum aggregate attendance across lectures and labs is needed to sit semester examinations.
  • 60–74% may be condoned only on medical grounds or approved deputation, with the Principal’s approval.
  • Absence of more than 3 days without prior leave needs a written explanation from a parent or guardian.

Examinations

  • Internal assessment includes mid-semester tests, assignments, class tests and attendance.
  • Unfair means: notes, phones or smart devices in the exam hall lead to paper cancellation and disciplinary referral.

Ragging

  • A cognizable criminal offence. Anyone found guilty faces expulsion, cancellation of admission and an FIR with the local police.
