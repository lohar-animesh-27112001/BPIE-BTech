Campus and facilities

Classrooms, laboratories, workshops, a library and student support spaces are ready at Siromonipur, with more being added in line with regulatory standards.

Academic spaces

Rooms for lectures, small-group work and practical sessions, set up for structured, student-friendly teaching.

Classrooms

Functional classrooms with enough seating and teaching boards for regular classes.

Tutorial rooms

Spaces for small-group discussion, doubt-clearing and interactive learning.

Laboratory rooms

Separate rooms for practical sessions across disciplines.

Seminar room

A seminar hall with a projector for talks and presentations.

Labs and workshops

Foundational lab and workshop facilities for core engineering practice. More equipment is being added as the programs grow.

Basic electrical engineering lab

Essential electrical instruments and experimental setups for fundamental experiments and circuit work.

Electrical Engineering

Mechanical workshop

Lathe, drilling and grinding machines for hands-on machining, tool handling and manufacturing practice.

Mechanical Engineering

Welding workshop

Basic welding equipment with safety provisions, for fabrication and metalwork skills.

Fabrication and metalwork

Foundational experiments in physics and chemistry that connect scientific principles to practice.

Physics and chemistry

Safety instructions are displayed in every lab and workshop, and lab coats and safety boots are required during practicals.

All labs and workshops

Computing and library

Digital access and study space, with the digital library now being set up.

Computer centre

Multiple desktop systems for programming, project work and digital learning.

Internet on campus

Networking infrastructure gives students access to online resources and academic content.

Library

Textbooks and reference books, with a quiet reading room for self-study.

Reading Room
