About the department

The curriculum will cover electrical machines, power systems, control systems and renewable energy, with an emphasis on practical exposure and problem-solving.

Basic electrical lab set-ups and instruments for initial practical learning are already in place. Details of faculty, laboratories and the full curriculum will be added after approval.

How the department teaches

Strong electrical fundamentals, practical understanding and analytical skills come first, taught through a balance of theory and hands-on lab work, in line with academic requirements and regulatory norms.

Areas of focus

Five subject areas run through the program, from first-year foundations to final-year projects.

Electrical machines and power systems
Control systems and instrumentation
Renewable energy technologies
Power electronics
Electrical design and applications
