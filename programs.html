What every program includes

The curriculum is being structured to AICTE guidelines and the affiliating university’s norms, with strong theory, practical work and industry-oriented learning.

Programs start from the 2026–27 session, subject to approval from the concerned authorities.
  • Core subjects and electives in each discipline, with laboratory work every semester
  • Project-based learning that applies theory to real problems
  • Practical, lab-based teaching in the computer centre, electrical lab, workshops and science lab
  • Industry-relevant curriculum framework, aligned with current academic and industry needs
  • Academic support through tutorial rooms, the library and a language lab for communication skills
